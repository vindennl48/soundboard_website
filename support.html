Support

Soundboard for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

Get help

Email zofiaudio@gmail.com with a description of the problem, your device, and its operating system version. We read every message.

How do I add a sound?

Tap an empty pad. Choose Record what's playing to capture audio from another app, or Load file… to pick an audio or video file.

How does recording work on iPhone and iPad?

  • Tap Record what's playing, then Start Broadcast on the system sheet.
  • After the countdown, switch to the app with your sound and play it.
  • Come back and tap Stop. The quiet start and end are trimmed for you.

iOS shows a message that the broadcast stopped; that is expected. Only audio is kept, never the screen.

My recording is silent

Some apps block their audio from being captured, especially music and video services with protected content. Try playing the sound somewhere else, such as a web page, and record again. Also check that the sound was actually playing out loud while recording.

Why does Android ask about the microphone and sharing my screen?

Android requires both permissions before any app can capture what the phone is playing. The app captures only the audio your phone plays; it does not record your voice or your screen.

How do I trim or change a sound?

Switch to Edit at the top and tap a pad. Drag the In and Out handles on the waveform, adjust volume, fades, pitch and speed, then tap Done.

How do I move my sounds to another device?

Open Settings, choose Export board…, and save the file. On the other device choose Import board… and pick that file.

Sounds don't play

Check the volume in the app's top bar and your device volume. On iPhone the app plays even with the silent switch on.
