Privacy Policy

Soundboard for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Operator: Mitchell Woodbine · zofiaudio@gmail.com

Summary

Soundboard plays short sounds from a grid of pads. It does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Everything the app does happens locally on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no servers — there is nowhere for your data to go.

Data we collect

We collect no data from the app. Soundboard does not send any information to Mitchell Woodbine or to any third party, and it makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage, and never leaves it unless you export it yourself:

  • Your sounds — audio you record or import, and the playable clips made from it.
  • Your boards — pad names, colours, emoji, pictures you choose, trim and effect settings, and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Preferences — settings such as volume and whether silence is trimmed automatically.

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting sounds or boards in the app, or by deleting the app.

Recording what your device plays

If you choose to record a sound, the app captures the audio your device is playing until you stop. The recording is saved only on your device.

  • iPhone and iPad: recording uses Apple's system screen broadcast. The app keeps only the audio; the picture is discarded immediately and never stored. The microphone is not used.
  • Android: recording uses Android's screen-capture permission, which the system describes as sharing your screen. The app captures only audio. Android also requires the microphone permission for this kind of capture; the app does not record from the microphone.
  • Mac: recording uses the macOS System Audio Recording permission.

Recording only happens when you start it, and the system shows an indicator while it is active.

Files and pictures you choose

When you load an audio file or choose a picture for a pad, the app copies that file into its own storage. It has no access to the rest of your files or your photo library.

Exporting

You can export a board as a single file. It is saved or shared only where you choose; the app does not upload it anywhere.

Third-party services

Soundboard uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
